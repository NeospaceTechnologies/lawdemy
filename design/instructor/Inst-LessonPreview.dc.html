What is a Contract?

A contract is a legally binding agreement between two or more parties that creates mutual obligations enforceable by law.

Core Elements of a Valid Contract

  • Offer: one party makes a clear proposal to enter into an agreement.
  • Acceptance: the other party must agree to the terms as presented, without modification.
  • Consideration: something of value must be exchanged between the parties.
  • Intention to create legal relations: the parties must intend the agreement to be legally binding.
  • Capacity: all parties must be legally capable of entering into a contract.
  • Legality of purpose: the contract’s subject matter must be lawful.

Common Types of Contracts

  • Lease agreements
  • Service contracts
  • Sales agreements
  • Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs)

Why It Matters

Understanding contracts helps you protect your rights, avoid disputes, and make informed decisions in business and everyday life.
