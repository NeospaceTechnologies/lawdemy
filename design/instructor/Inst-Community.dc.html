Announcement Guidelines

Keep learners informed about official course-related updates. Please follow these rules.

Monthly Limit: You can publish up to 4 announcements per course every month.

Post Types Allowed: Announcements should be used for:

  • Adding new modules or lessons
  • Reordering or renaming course content
  • Changes to assessment materials
  • Course maintenance or temporary unavailability

Attachments: You may include JPEG or PNG files (max 5MB) relevant to your update.

Multi-Course Targeting: One announcement can be sent to multiple published courses.

No External Events/Webinars: Posts about live sessions or off-platform events are not allowed, to keep learner retention within the platform.

Note

  • Learners receive in-app notifications when a new announcement is published for any course they’re enrolled in.
  • Announcements are not currently sent via email, to keep communication focused within the platform.
